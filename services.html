Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to clean my recyclables before pickup?

Yes, please quickly rinse plastics and metals to remove food residue. This prevents contamination of the entire batch.

What happens if I miss my scheduled pickup?

You can easily reschedule through our mobile app or dashboard for the next available route in your area at no extra charge.

Can you handle construction debris?

Yes, our bulk waste and roll-off dumpster services are perfect for construction sites. We also provide LEED-compliant recycling reports.

Are the smart bin sensors secure?

Absolutely. Our IoT sensors use end-to-end encryption and transmit only fill-level data over cellular networks.
